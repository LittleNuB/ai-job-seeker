## What You Can Try

1. Create an account or log in.
2. Open "JD 解析" and paste a job description.
3. Review the inferred role, visible requirements, hidden needs, and interview focus.
4. Use that JD to start a resume match, or open "简历匹配" directly.
5. Paste or upload a resume, choose a target position, and run the match analysis.
6. Review the score, advantages, gaps, and improvement plan.
7. Try exporting a report from the result page.
8. Open "历史记录" to confirm your analyses are saved.
9. Try the AI follow-up panel if you want to ask about a result.
10. Open "账号中心" to export your data or delete your account.

## Privacy And Data Notes

Please do not upload information you do not want used for this trial. Resume, JD, and chat content may be sent to a third-party AI model provider to generate results.

For safer testing:

- Remove ID numbers, home address, private phone numbers, and unnecessary personal details.
- Use a shortened or anonymized resume if you only want to test functionality.
- Do not upload confidential company documents.
- Use account deletion if you want your trial account and associated stored records removed.

## What Feedback Helps Most

Please tell us:

- Which workflow you tried.
- Whether the result was useful or too generic.
- Whether the match score felt reasonable.
- Whether the result cited convincing evidence from your resume/JD.
- Whether the waiting, retry, upload, history, or export experience was confusing.
- Any error message, blank state, or slow response you saw.
- Browser and device if something broke.

## Known Trial Limits

- The model can be wrong or overconfident.
- Scores are directional references, not objective hiring decisions.
- Long resumes or long JDs may take longer or time out.
- Some position data may still be incomplete while the job database is being expanded.
- This trial does not include billing, team accounts, or production-scale support.
