# AI Job Copilot

AI Job Copilot helps a candidate turn ordinary, under-described experience into the strongest job-specific story they can comfortably explain in an interview. Candidate-provided statements are accepted as the working premise; the product does not ask for external proof or perform background checks.

## Language

**Target Application**:
One bounded preparation task for a concrete role or job description.
_Avoid_: Career plan, path, role prediction

**Role Signal**:
One capability, result, or pattern of ownership that the target job is likely to reward. It may be explicit in the JD or a separately labeled interpretation.
_Avoid_: Keyword count, recruiter truth

**Base Fact**:
A statement the candidate supplies about what they did, decided, influenced, or achieved. It is accepted as the working input without demanding documentary proof.
_Avoid_: Verified evidence, objective ground truth

**Achievement Lead**:
A promising but under-developed part of the candidate's experience that may become a stronger job-relevant claim after clarification.
_Avoid_: Missing capability, proven achievement

**Clarifying Question**:
A short, adaptive question used to recover ownership, difficulty, scale, method, comparison, or outcome that the candidate has not yet expressed.
_Avoid_: Audit request, evidence demand

**Defensible Amplification**:
Selecting, translating, compressing, and strengthening candidate-provided experience so its market value is easier to recognize, without silently adding a new material fact.
_Avoid_: Fact verification, unrestricted fabrication

**Expression Strength**:
The degree of assertiveness used to frame the same Base Facts. The current modes are `Steady`, `Competitive`, and `Stretch`; a stronger mode changes emphasis and ownership language, not the underlying history.
_Avoid_: Formal/casual tone, confidence score

**Resume Claim**:
A job-specific bullet, summary statement, or project description produced from Base Facts and the candidate's answers.
_Avoid_: Source quotation, hiring promise

**Open Detail**:
A material detail that would make a Resume Claim stronger but has not been supplied yet, such as a number, scope, decision right, or outcome. The product asks or marks it for editing rather than inventing it silently.
_Avoid_: Evidence gap, disqualification

**Interview Story**:
A concise explanation of the context, action, trade-off, and result behind an accepted Resume Claim, including likely follow-up questions.
_Avoid_: Proof package, scripted lie

**User Decision**:
The candidate's selection, correction, strengthening, or rejection of a proposed Resume Claim.
_Avoid_: External verification

**Targeted Resume Version**:
The final resume content selected for one Target Application, together with reusable Interview Stories and unresolved Open Details.
_Avoid_: Career roadmap, guaranteed application package
